VLA Data Collection, Fine-Tuning, and Real-Robot Experiments

2025.04 — 2025.09 · Robotics Institute, Zhejiang University

Model reproduction, training, and deployment

Collected teleoperation data, fine-tuned models, and deployed on Franka for grasping and multi-step, long-horizon manipulation. Reproduced π0 and π0.5 and explored robot learning methods including HIL-SERL and ConRFT.

Main Contributions

  • Reproduced models and algorithms including π0, π0.5, ACT, HIL-SERL, and ConRFT.
  • Handled teleoperation data collection, fine-tuning, inference deployment, and real-robot experiments.
  • Built a lightweight VLA prototype using a pretrained VLM and an action expert, exploring grasping and manipulation with limited compute.

π0 Fine-Tuning · Long-Horizon Tidying
